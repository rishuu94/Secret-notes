About

Secure Notes Manager v1.0 — A browser-based encrypted note-taking app.
Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript. Encryption via CryptoJS (AES-256).

⚠️ Security note: This is a client-side application. The encryption key is derived from your master password and kept in memory during your session. Anyone with physical access to your browser's localStorage can see encrypted data (but not the plaintext without your password). Do not use this for highly sensitive information like financial credentials.
